            and it is our element to delete.

  the size sweep — fit bypass128, bypass360, bypass (1280x720) and bypass14
  (2560x1440) as ms/frame against bytes/frame:
    the SLOPE is the part of the journey that a different transport could
    remove. The INTERCEPT is the worker's own per-frame work, and no
    transport touches it.
    for scale, the pipe measures ~1.1 GB/s and a frame crosses it three
    times in this loop (our write, the worker's read, our read of the
    result), so a purely byte-bound journey would slope at about 2.7ms per
    MB: 14.7MB would be ~40ms of pipe traffic, and 128x128 (64KB) would be
    nearly zero. A large intercept means an own-host build cannot pay for
    itself, and that the ceiling is not the transport at all.
